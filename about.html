My name is Brett Gustin, and I am a Data Analyst based outside Syracuse, New York. I graduated from Syracuse University with a B.A. in Sport Analytics and a minor in Information Management & Technology. I currently work for the Arizona Cardinals as an Analyst, Partnership Solutions, where I develop reporting and generate insights that support corporate partnership strategy and decision-making. Prior to joining the Cardinals, I worked for the Detroit Tigers in Baseball Systems Operations, leveraging biomechanical data and performance analytics to support player development and evaluation. I also worked with the New York Mets at their Triple-A affiliate in Syracuse, utilizing Hawkeye and TrackMan technologies to analyze player and game performance. Earlier in my career, I served as a Technology Analyst at SIDEARM Sports, where I developed key performance indicators to monitor and improve server performance and operational efficiency. I am passionate about applying data analytics, data science, and machine learning to transform complex information into actionable insights that drive smarter business decisions.
